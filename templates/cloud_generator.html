Generation Options

Configure cloud of points generation parameters

Interior Clouds of Points

Generate clouds of points inside internal regions (holes). Recommended for complex geometries with internal boundaries.

Better accuracy Complex geometries

Reduce Contour Points

Reduces contour points by applying the reduction algorithm multiple times. Higher values provide more aggressive reduction for very dense datasets.

Faster processing Large datasets Configurable reduction

Generation Method

Choose how points are distributed in your cloud. Regular distribution creates uniform, grid-like patterns, while Natural distribution creates more organic, randomly spaced patterns.
